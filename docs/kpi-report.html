Brezzaluce Energia: simulated KPI tree

55 simulated calls, weighted by the assumed contact mix. Synthetic data: a method demo, not results from a deployment.

  • North star: cost per resolved contact: €1.43 on per-minute pricing, €2.19 on per-resolution pricing
    • Safety gate: PASS (100% of calls pass every check)
      • none
    • Task success: 97%
      • Containment (no human): 82%
      • Resolution: 70%
      • Transfers by reason
        • auth_failed: 5%
        • customer_request: 10%
        • system_error: 3%
    • Effort: 1.9 caller turns per call, about 1.3 min handle time
    • 1,000 contacts: €1,002.00 per-minute vs €1,530.00 per-resolution (operator cost included)
    • Break-even: per-resolution pricing is cheaper while the resolution rate stays below 11%

Assumptions

  • seconds_per_exchange = 25.0
  • fixed_overhead_seconds = 30.0
  • price_per_minute_eur = 0.08
  • price_per_resolution_eur = 0.9
